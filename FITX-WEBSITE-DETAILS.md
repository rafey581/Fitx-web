## 7. Design & Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Primary Accent (Brand Orange)**: `#FF4B00` - Used for CTAs, highlights, and accents
- **Navy/Dark**: `#1E2330` - Used for dark sections and navigation
- **Ink/Near Black**: `#0D0A10` - Used for footer and body text
- **Accent Variants**: Derived tones follow the primary accent automatically (softer, deeper, glows)

### Typography
- **Headings**: Bold condensed display font (Montserrat/heading font) - All caps, heavy weight (800-900), tight tracking
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif for readability
- **Accent Text**: Serif font used for select quotes and elegant touches

### Visual Style
- **Theme**: Bold, high-energy gym aesthetic
- **Dark Bands**: Arena-style dark sections with grid texture and accent glows
- **Gradients**: Orange gradients on buttons and highlight elements
- **Cards**: Clean cards with hover lift effects
- **Full-Bleed Hero**: Large impactful hero section with overlays
- **Image-Optional Design**: Site looks polished even without photos thanks to gradient placeholders and CSS-based decorative elements
- **Animations**: Subtle fade-up animations on content (respects reduced motion preferences)
- **Mobile-First**: Responsive design across all devices
